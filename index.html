The concept

Your third place, between home and work.

Coffee Cozy will be a small, warm café where students, remote workers, and neighbors can stay a while. Think soft lighting, comfortable seats, fast Wi-Fi, and coffee made with care, at prices that make it an everyday habit instead of a treat.

  • Cozy by design. Warm wood, plants, and seating made for long stays.
  • Quality you can afford. Locally roasted beans and fair, everyday prices.
  • Made for the neighborhood. Study nights, small events, and local suppliers.
Who it's for Students · Remote workers · Families · Coffee lovers

The plan

From idea to opening day.

  1. Now

    Planning & partnerships

    Finalize the business plan, menu, and budget, and secure funding partners.

    Location & permits

    Build-out & hiring

  5. Q4 2027

    Grand opening

    Officially open our doors, with launch promos for early supporters.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Coffee Cozy already open?

Not yet. We're in the planning and funding stage, with a target opening in 2027. Sign up below to get updates.

Where will the café be?

We're looking for a space in Quezon City, near schools and offices, where people would love a cozy place to study, work, and meet.

How are profits shared with a business partner?

It depends on what we agree on, such as how much capital each person puts in and who runs the café. Everything is written in a partnership agreement before any money changes hands.

Is there a minimum amount to partner?

No fixed minimum. Crowdfunding pledges start small, while business partners and investors usually discuss amounts with us directly.

Can I see the full business plan?

Yes. Send us a message, and we'll share the business plan, budget, and projections and schedule a call.
